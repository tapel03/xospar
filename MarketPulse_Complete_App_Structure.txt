MARKETPULSE — COMPLETE APP STRUCTURE
========================================

Purpose
-------
MarketPulse is a mobile market-intelligence and trading application combining:
- Forex
- Stocks
- Commodities
- Crypto
- Global market map
- Market/news intelligence
- AI analysis
- Economic calendar
- Watchlist
- Alerts
- MT4/MT5 trading-account integration
- TradingView-style charting
- User trade visualization

16. TRADING CHART + USER TRADES
==============================

The chart is the bridge between market analysis and the user's real trades.

Example:

EUR/USD
1.18240

       TP ───────────────────── 1.19000

                         Current
                           │
                           ●

       Entry ──────────── ● 1.18120
                           │
                           │

       SL ──────────────── 1.17800


The chart can display:
- Candles
- Indicators
- Drawing tools
- Entry price
- Current price
- Stop loss
- Take profit
- Trade direction
- Trade size
- P/L

18. CORE PRINCIPLE
==================

Home tells me what matters.

Markets lets me investigate it.

The Chart lets me analyze it.

My Trades shows what I have actually traded.

MT4/MT5 provides my trading-account information.

Calendar tells me what is coming.

News explains what is happening.

The Global Map shows what is happening around the world.

AI explains the information and provides analysis.

More lets me manage everything.

The application therefore remains simple at the top level while supporting a sophisticated
market-intelligence and trading experience underneath.
